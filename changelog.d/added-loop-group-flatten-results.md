- ForEach Group and ForLoop Group have a **Flatten Results** setting. When each iteration adds a
  list, Results comes out as one flat list instead of a list of lists. **Flatten Depth** sets how
  many levels to flatten (0 for all), and strings and dicts stay whole.
  [#6315](https://github.com/griptape-ai/griptape-nodes-engine/issues/6315)
